## Key Features

- **Camera:** 1-inch 50MP CMOS sensor, capable of 4K/60fps HDR video and 4K/120fps slow-motion capture  
- **Portability:** Weighs under 250 grams, making it travel-friendly and easy to carry  
- **Gimbal:** 225° rotating gimbal for versatile shooting angles, including true vertical shooting  
- **Flight Time:** Up to 52 minutes with the Plus battery, ideal for extended shooting sessions  
- **Intelligent Flight Modes:** Includes MasterShots, FocusTrack, and QuickShots for automated creative shots  
- **Obstacle Sensing:** Omnidirectional LiDAR and Nightscape for safe flying even in low light conditions  
- **Controller:** Comes with DJI RC 2 smart controller for responsive control and clear live view  

## Who Should Use It?

- Travel photographers and videographers looking for a lightweight drone  
- Content creators and vloggers needing vertical video capabilities  
- Real estate agents for capturing property aerial tours  
- Beginners who want an accessible yet high-performance drone
